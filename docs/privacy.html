Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026. Built under Apple's core principle that privacy is a fundamental human right.

1. Zero Telemetry & Zero Data Collection

SSD Photo Vault does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, analytics, diagnostics, location history, or photos. We have no user accounts, no login portals, and no remote servers.

2. Direct Storage Routing (No Cloud Storage)

When you export photos and videos, the data streams directly from your local Apple Photos library directly into your chosen external storage device (SSD, flash drive, or local folder). Data is never routed through proxies, cloud staging, or third-party servers.

3. 100% On-Device Apple Neural Engine Intelligence

All smart curation (blur detection, duplicate removal, and chronological grouping) executes entirely on your device's Apple Neural Engine. No cloud AI services are ever contacted.

4. Non-Destructive Read-Only PhotoKit Access

SSD Photo Vault requests read-only authorization to extract original image and video bytes. It never modifies, compresses, overwrites, or deletes files in your device's photo library.

5. Official Apple Privacy Manifest Declarations

  • NSPrivacyTracking: false (We never track users across third-party apps or websites).
  • NSPrivacyTrackingDomains: Empty list.
  • NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes: Empty list (Zero data categories collected).
