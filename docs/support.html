How Can We Help You?

Frequently asked questions, drive setup instructions, and direct support assistance.

1. Connecting External Storage to iPhone or iPad

SSD Photo Vault is designed to work with all USB-C mass storage devices:

  • Supported Drives: Samsung T7, T9, SanDisk Extreme, Crucial X6/X8/X9, Kingston, external NVMe SSD enclosures, and USB-C flash drives.
  • File Formats: APFS and ExFAT are recommended for high-speed writes. MS-DOS (FAT32) is supported up to 4 GB per video file. NTFS is read-only on iOS.
  • Lightning iPhones: Use an official Apple Lightning-to-USB3 Camera Adapter and connect external power via the Lightning charging port to supply bus power to the drive.

2. Restoring Purchases

If you upgraded to SSD Photo Vault Pro and switched devices or reinstalled:

  • Open SSD Photo Vault → tap the Settings gear icon in the top right.
  • Scroll to the Membership & Unlocks section.
  • Tap Restore Purchases.
  • Your receipt will be validated with the App Store immediately at no charge.

3. Verifying Cryptographic Checksums

Every time photos are archived, a .photosnap/Vault_Manifest.csv and verification report Vault_Certificate.txt are saved to the root of your external drive. You can also run the Verify Vault Integrity shortcut via Siri anytime to independently check every file on the drive against its recorded SHA-256 hash.

4. Privacy & Permissions

SSD Photo Vault requests read-only photo library access. It never collects, transmits, or uploads any data. All smart curation executes entirely on your device's Apple Neural Engine.
