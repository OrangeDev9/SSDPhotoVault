Customer Support & Help Center

Have questions or need assistance with your SSD Photo Vault? We're here to help.

Which external drives and cables work best?
Any USB-C drive formatted as APFS or ExFAT works natively with iPhone 15/16/17 Series, iPad, and Mac. For Lightning iPhones, use an Apple Lightning-to-USB3 Camera Adapter with connected external wall power.
Does SSD Photo Vault alter or delete photos from Apple Photos?
Never. SSD Photo Vault operates with strictly read-only PhotoKit access. It never deletes, overwrites, modifies, or compresses any media in your original library.
How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?
Open Settings (gear icon in the top right of the welcome screen) → tap Restore Purchases. Your Apple ID will instantly re-validate your Pro membership across all your devices.
